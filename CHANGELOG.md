# Changelog

## 0.1.1

- First CurseForge release. Tuned defaults (frame size 80, Oswald font, FORCED SQUARE countdown), bundled Oswald font, drag box and DR badge fixes.

## 0.1.0

First release for TBC Anniversary (interface 20506).

- Enemy arena frames with class-colored health, power bar and a live 3D portrait.
- Portrait takeover: active cast with a cast bar (three styles, interrupt flash), or the active CC with a big countdown. Silences and roots show too.
- Diminishing-returns slots per category with green, yellow, orange, red borders and reset countdown, tracked through DRList-1.0.
- PvP trinket and Will of the Forsaken tracking with a frame flash and cooldown countdown.
- Click casting: modifier plus mouse button bindings to target, focus, spell or macro.
- Settings panel with a Welcome tab, layout (scale, growth direction, spacing, lock), bar texture, fonts, profiles.
- Test mode with 2 to 5 fake opponents.
- Hides the default Blizzard arena frames.
